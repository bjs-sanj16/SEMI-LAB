P_dissipation = I_rms² × R. At 75.0% duty cycle and 350mA peak pulse current, average thermal dissipation is 0.68W, requiring a 2W flameproof metal oxide resistor to ensure thermal safety margin.

memory MOSFET Gate Charge Dynamics

NE555 output stage can source up to 200mA, rapidly charging the STD30NF06L Q_g (total gate charge 30nC) in ~150ns, keeping switching transition losses (E_sw) under 2.4 milliwatts.

center_focus_strong Stroboscopic Flicker Elimination

Operating at 1.44 kHz exceeds the Critical Flicker Fusion (CFF) threshold (60–90 Hz) and video rolling shutter artifacts, providing high-CRI illumination compliant with IEEE 1789 standards.
