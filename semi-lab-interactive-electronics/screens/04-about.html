Bridging Pure Solid-State Physics with Benchtop Hardware

Standard electronics coursework frequently bifurcates into mathematical abstractions of semiconductor equations or disconnected cookbook schematics. Semi Lab collapses this divide. By placing interactive circuit simulation alongside exact junction physics, users observe how minority carrier diffusion in steering diodes alters duty timing down to the microsecond.

Every waveform rendered in our oscilloscope view directly correlates with real-world component parasitics, from Gate-Source capacitance (Ciss) in N-channel power switches to inductive back-EMF generated by high-RPM brushless windings.
