HARDWARE SNAPSHOT

Dual Diode Astable Timing Circuit

The 1N4148 diodes steer current through alternate segments of the 100k potentiometer, granting independent charge (T1) and discharge (T2) times without altering overall frequency.

POWER STAGE

Logic-Level N-Channel Gate Drive

Driving the gate of an IRLZ44N directly from pin 3 yields instant transition between cut-off and saturation, eliminating thermal runaways seen in conventional linear rheostats.
