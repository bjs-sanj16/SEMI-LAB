Cycle Kinetics & Steering Equations

Capacitor Charging Cycle (T_ON)

Current leaves Pin 3 or VCC, passing forward through steering diode D1 and charging resistor R_A directly into capacitor C1.

Time Duration Formula T_on = 0.693 × R_A × C_1 Steering Diode D2 remains reverse-biased
Pin 3 Output: HIGH (12V)
trending_down

Capacitor Discharge Cycle (T_OFF)

When Vc reaches 2/3 VCC, Upper Comp sets the latch. The discharge transistor opens Pin 7 to ground. Capacitor C1 discharges through R_B and forward diode D2 into Pin 7.

Time Duration Formula T_off = 0.693 × R_B × C_1 Diode D1 isolates R_A during drain sink
Pin 3 Output: LOW (0V)
tune

Independent Duty Ratio (D)

Standard 555 astables cannot fall below a 50% duty cycle. By introducing opposed steering diodes D1 & D2, the charge and discharge paths decouple completely:

Uncoupled Duty Formula D = R_A / (R_A + R_B) Permits full 1% to 99% variable sweep
Frequency: 1.44 / [(Ra + Rb) × C1]
