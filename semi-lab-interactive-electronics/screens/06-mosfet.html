functions Analytical Formulations
1. SATURATION REGIME ACTIVE
ID = ½ · μnCox · (W/L) · (VGS - VTH)²

Occurs when VDS ≥ (VGS - VTH). The conductive channel pinches off at the drain side. Drain current saturates into a constant plateau, rendering the MOSFET an ideal voltage-controlled current source.

2. TRIODE / LINEAR REGIME INACTIVE
ID = μnCox · (W/L) · [(VGS - VTH)VDS - ½VDS²]

Active when VDS < (VGS - VTH). The electron channel spans continuously from source to drain, functioning as a voltage-controlled variable resistor ideal for low-loss synchronous rectification.

3. CONDUCTION RESISTANCE OHMIC LIMIT
RDS(on) = 1 / [μnCox · (W/L) · (VGS - VTH)]

Governs heat dissipation and conduction loss in switching converter stages. Driving VGS substantially beyond VTH minimizes channel bulk resistance and prevents thermal runaway.

ENGINEERING SUMMARY

Why Gate Overdrive Matters in High-Frequency PWM

Operating near threshold (VGS ≈ VTH) leads to massive switching losses because the channel resistance spikes parabolically. Driving the gate with 10V to 12V guarantees complete inversion, forcing the MOSFET into deep triode during "ON" states with minimal voltage drops.
