MISSION & PEDAGOGY

Bridging Pure Solid-State Physics with Benchtop Hardware

Standard electronics coursework frequently bifurcates into mathematical abstractions of semiconductor equations or disconnected cookbook schematics. Semi Lab collapses this divide. By placing interactive circuit simulation alongside exact junction physics, users observe how minority carrier diffusion in steering diodes slows duty timing down to the microsecond.

Every waveform rendered in our oscilloscope view directly correlates with real-world component parasitic resistances, from gate source capacitance (Qg) in N-channel power switches to inductive back-EMF generated by high-RPM brushless windings.

TOPOLOGY OVERVIEW

Integrated Closed Loop

The NE555 operates in astable configuration with charge and discharge paths segregated via counter-facing fast diodes, ensuring constant frequency while adjusting PWM duty cycle smoothly.

Did you know? In extreme high-power applications (such as electric vehicles and grid inverters), silicon MOSFETs are increasingly replaced by Silicon Carbide (SiC) and Gallium Nitride (GaN) wide-bandgap semiconductors. These modern devices have breakdown electric fields 10× higher than traditional silicon, enabling switching frequencies in the megahertz range with even lower on-resistance!
