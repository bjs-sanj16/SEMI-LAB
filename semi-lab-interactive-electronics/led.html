Perceived vs Measured Brightness (Stevens' Law)

CIE 1931 Lightness
■ Perceived Brightness (Eye: B ≈ D^0.4) ■ Measured Radiant Flux (Linear: L ∝ D)
Did you know? Human vision response to light intensity is non-linear and approximately logarithmic (following Stevens' Power Law: B ∝ L0.4). At only 25% PWM duty cycle, the human eye perceives the light as nearly 60% bright! In contrast, varying current linearly with a resistor shifts the LED's spectral wavelength and color temperature, which is why PWM dimming is universally preferred in cinema and surgical lighting.
