How the Circuit Operates

From manual knob rotation to sub-micron electron channel conduction, trace the complete energy transformation path.

1

1. Set Duty Cycle

The rotary potentiometer shifts the ratio between charging resistor Ra and discharging resistor Rb. Two opposed diodes (1N4148) steer current into separate pathways.

2

2. 555 Makes the Pulses

Timing capacitor C1 continuously ramps between 13 Vcc and 23 Vcc. Internal comparators toggle an SR flip-flop, creating a steady 1.44 kHz square wave at Pin 3.

3

3. MOSFET Switches

Pin 3 drives the gate of an N-channel power MOSFET (IRFZ44N). High gate voltage attracts electrons to form a conductive inversion channel with negligible on-resistance (Rds(on) ≈ 17.5 mΩ).

4

4. Load Spins or Dims

The load receives rapid pulses of full supply voltage. The fan motor's mechanical inertia or the human eye's retinal persistence filters these pulses into a smooth, efficient average output.

Did you know? Traditional linear series regulators (like LM7805 or variable rheostats) drop excess voltage directly across the transistor as heat: Ploss = Iload × (Vin - Vout). In contrast, our PWM circuit switches fully ON (where voltage drop is near zero) or fully OFF (where current is zero), achieving well over 90% to 95% electrical efficiency and keeping the MOSFET completely cool!
